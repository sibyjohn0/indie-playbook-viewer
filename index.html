Step 1 of 2 — Your Artist Type
🎤
Singer-Songwriter / Vocalist
One voice, one instrument, one song. You own both the composition and the master recording. The most crowded category — and the one with the clearest identity advantage.
↑ Dual rights ownership. Life + 60yr royalty asset.
🎸
Instrumentalist / Musician
Technical mastery, visual performance, unique sonic identity. Your music is disproportionately valuable for sync licensing — OTT platforms need you more than Bollywood does.
↑ Sync-first. YouTube education flywheel. Session income.
🎛
Producer / Beatmaker
You are the architectural engine of modern music. You can generate income from beat licensing before you have any public audience. The challenge: invisibility. The solution: build your sound as a brand.
↑ Beat licensing income before audience. Sound-as-brand.
